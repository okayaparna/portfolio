Introduction

Introducing Phia

Phia is a fashion shopping and discovery app that uses AI to help users find clothing and style recommendations based on their browsing patterns, saved items, and style signals.

Reimagining rewards as access, impact, and experience — a rewards program that actually pays off.

Phia app screens — shopping feed, outfits for you, and style stories
The Problem

Everyone likes to shop, not everyone likes to spend.

There is a collective resistance towards consumption due to the conditions of the economy. How to maintain regular consumer behavior without promoting over-consumption?

Phia’s philosophy lies in sustainable and intentional shopping. How can they help users make smarter purchasing decisions?

Currently, Phia’s consumers are largely shopaholics. How can they tap into other parts of the fashion community?

Three key challenges for Phia’s rewards program
Insights & Strategy

Understanding the rewards & subscriptions landscape

In order to keep every type of shopper in mind, a new rewards program at Phia would have to grow into an ecosystem and tap into community through other entry points.

Rewards ecosystem — IRL activations, community favorites, and points as purchasing power

What does the ideal rewards program look like?

User research surfaced key themes: mindful consumption habits, the desire for unique activations and experiences, and the power of community to attract new users organically beyond just monetary benefits.

User research quotes about shopping habits and rewards expectations

Tiers enhance specific features for those who value them most.

Since many rewards programs are standard and lack customization, new users might opt out if they feel they aren’t getting the most value.

Phia Free vs Phia Pro tier comparison
Solutions

Demand Forecasting

Before adding to cart, users are prompted to take a look at the trend analysis data provided by Phia.

By predicting the demand of an item, Phia can determine if an item is worth waiting for or purchasing right on the spot.

Demand Forecasting — product detail screen with trend analysis and typical price data

A personalized Rewards feed that allows you to give back in more ways than one.

Through personalized rewards feeds, shoppers can spend their points on engaging with the fashion community around them, discovering new brands, and even gifting their friends.

Fashion Community members earn visibility while shoppers gain access

Stylists, fashion-creators and other members of the fashion community can share items in their closet as looks, allowing shoppers to expand their range of search, while also gaining traction through increased discovery.

Fashion community stylist profile

Intention applies to systems just as it does to products.

Users on the free tier can enjoy a 14-day free trial, unlocking rewards and offerings from the Pro tier.

The goal is not to pressure users into a paid plan with unrealistic promises or encourage over-consumption, but to offer them a chance to make intentional choices for a better-curated feed.
