Concept

Overview

An immersive gaming experience that dives into afterlife from a Buddhist lens. The project explores themes of impermanence, karma, and rebirth through interactive storytelling and environmental design.

1.1 — Game Environment
World Building

Process

Built immersive 3D environments in Blender and Unity that reflect Buddhist cosmology. Each realm represents a different state of existence, with visual language drawing from traditional iconography reinterpreted through a contemporary digital aesthetic.

Character & Interaction Design

Mechanics

Designed gameplay mechanics that mirror Buddhist principles — player choices carry karmic weight, affecting which realms they traverse and how the narrative unfolds.

2.2 — Gameplay Mechanics
Final Experience

Outcome

A meditative, visually rich gaming experience that invites players to reflect on life, death, and what comes after — presented at Parsons School of Design.
