Celebrating the legacy of fashion and design at Parsons School of Design's annual benefit. The 75th edition required a visual identity that honored tradition while feeling fresh and contemporary.

Process

Designed a comprehensive brand system for the benefit event, spanning invitations, programs, signage, digital assets, and stage graphics. The system balanced Parsons' institutional heritage with a bold visual statement.

A cohesive visual experience that elevated the benefit's presence across all touchpoints, from pre-event marketing to on-site environmental graphics and post-event documentation.
