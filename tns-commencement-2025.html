Brief

Overview

Celebrating Unity through Intersectionality at The New School's 89th Commencement. Designing a visual identity system that honors the diversity of graduates while creating a cohesive ceremonial experience.

1.1 — Commencement Visual Identity
Visual System

Process

Developed a modular design system that could flex across print, digital, and environmental applications — from stage graphics and programs to social media assets and wayfinding signage.

Motion & Environmental

Motion Design

Created animated title sequences and stage visuals in After Effects that brought the identity to life during the ceremony, reinforcing the theme of intersecting paths and shared celebration.

2.2 — Stage Graphics & Motion
Event Documentation

Outcome

A unified visual experience that accompanied over 3,000 graduates across all five colleges of The New School, celebrated at the ceremony venue.
